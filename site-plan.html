Impact Digital Academy

Website Development Plan

Site Name

Impact Digital Academy or alternative domain: impactdigitalacademy.com

This name was selected because it clearly communicates the organization's purpose: creating impact through digital education in an academy format. The name is professional, memorable, and directly relates to the mission of bridging digital skill gaps in underserved communities. The ".com" domain extension reinforces the educational focus.

Site Purpose

The Impact Digital Academy website will serve as the primary digital platform to:

  • Showcase the academy's mission to bridge digital skill gaps in underserved communities
  • Provide detailed information about available courses (Digital Marketing, Data Analytics, UX/UI Design)
  • Share success stories from alumni to build credibility and trust
  • Offer valuable resources including industry trend articles, downloadable guides, and webinar announcements
  • Facilitate inquiries and course registrations through user-friendly forms and portals
  • Establish the academy as a thought leader in digital skills education

Scenarios

These scenarios represent questions from potential students and partners visiting the website:

Scenario 1: Prospective Student

"I'm looking to transition into a digital marketing career but don't have any formal experience. What courses does Impact Digital Academy offer that would help me get started, and what kind of job placement support do you provide after completion?"

This scenario helps drive content around course details, prerequisites, career outcomes, and alumni success stories.

Scenario 2: Community Partner

"Our nonprofit organization works with underserved youth and we're interested in partnering with Impact Digital Academy. How can we collaborate to provide digital skills training to our community members?"

This scenario informs content about partnership opportunities, community programs, and contact channels for organizational inquiries.

Scenario 3: Industry Professional

"As a hiring manager in tech, I want to know if Impact Digital Academy graduates have the practical skills needed for entry-level UX/UI positions. What projects do students complete during the program and can I see examples of their work?"

This scenario drives content around portfolio showcases, curriculum details, and industry validation of the program quality.

Typography

Two fonts have been selected to create a clean, modern, and readable typographic system:

Montserrat (Headings)

Montserrat is a geometric sans-serif font that communicates modernity, professionalism, and clarity. It will be used for all headings (H1-H6) and the logo to establish strong visual hierarchy.

Open Sans (Body Text)

Open Sans is a humanist sans-serif font optimized for legibility on screens. It will be used for all body text, paragraphs, lists, and form inputs to ensure comfortable reading experiences across devices.

Typography Scale

  • H1: 2.5rem Montserrat Bold
  • H2: 1.8rem Montserrat Bold
  • H3: 1.4rem Montserrat Semibold
  • Body: 1rem Open Sans Regular
